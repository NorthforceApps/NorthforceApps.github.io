Northforce Apps

Useful Android apps, free to use.

Every app is free to use, with an optional Pro upgrade when you want more. Location, camera, and microphone stay on the phone unless you turn on sharing or export it yourself. Each app's privacy page says exactly what it collects.

  • GPS Logger Site Play Private route recorder and daily timeline. GPX in and out, screen-off logging, live share without a server-side trail.
  • CueLoop Site Play Gentle recurring cues on phone and Wear OS — hydration, posture, breaks — on your cadence.
  • RemoteMic Site Play Turn the phone into a wireless microphone for a speaker, a browser on Wi-Fi, or a hotspot.
  • WebCam Site Play Phone as a Wi-Fi camera. Browser, VLC or OBS over RTSP. No cloud.
  • Camera Sudoku Site Play Scan a paper Sudoku with the camera and solve it with step-by-step hints. Recognition runs on the phone; ad-supported, Pro removes ads.
